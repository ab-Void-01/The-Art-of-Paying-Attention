Fear Of Allah (God)

Fear Of Allah (God)

On the 26th of September, around 11:45 at night, a thought suddenly came to my mind: there is a name of Allah, Ya Samad, which is one of His most powerful and merciful names. As soon as it came to my mind, I became so afraid that I started asking God, “Ya Allah, mujhe bacha le” (Ya Allah, protect me). I was wondering what would happen if she asked my Allah and her prayer was accepted; then I would be doomed. I thought that He is so merciful that He could turn a stone into a glacier if He willed. It is kind of funny, but honestly, I was asking for forgiveness.

Imagination World

Imagination is better: you can have everything in your imagination world

Talking about imagination, let me tell you a true story about a boy who fell in love when he was in the 9th standard. My friends and I were standing at the cycle shed just before class started, around 9:00 AM. We were discussing something when a girl suddenly entered through the cycle shed gate. The shy boy fell in love with a single glimpse of her. He did not even realise that he said “WOW” when he saw her. That was the first word that came to his lips in his life after seeing a girl.

Time passed by: hours, days, months, and years. I fell so deeply in love that she stayed in my mind 24/7. Even at night, I used to listen to music and think about her until 1:00 AM. During school hours, I would only watch her from a distance when she came out for the break or half-time.

In my imagination, I created a scene in which I kidnapped her and took her to the jungle of my state, then set her free, but blindfolded. There, I was the hero who saved her and helped her escape from the forest. I was both the hero and the villain of the same story, just to make her fall in love with me. It is kind of funny, but that is who I am. I am telling you this so that you can understand: you should never force anyone. If you love something, let it be free.

Keep Moving Forward

Be Patient, Keep Imagining

The moral of the story is this: if you cannot get something, be patient. You will get it when the right time comes. Until then, keep imagining.
