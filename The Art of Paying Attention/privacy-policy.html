Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 08, 2026

The Void is a small, independently written journal. This page explains what little information is collected when you visit, why it's collected, and the choices you have about it. In short: no accounts, no ad trackers, no data sold to anyone — just a quiet corner of the internet doing its best to stay that way.

01Information we collect

The Void does not require you to create an account or log in to read anything published here. Information is only collected in a couple of narrow situations:

  • When you reach out. If you email a story submission or a message through the site's contact links, the site receives whatever you choose to include — typically your email address, name (if given), and the content of your message.
  • Basic technical data. Like most websites hosted on modern infrastructure, the hosting provider may log standard technical information (such as approximate location, browser type, and pages visited) for security and performance purposes. This site does not run separate analytics or advertising trackers layered on top of that.

02How it's used

Any information shared with The Void is used only to:

  • Read and respond to messages or story submissions;
  • Keep the site running securely and diagnose the occasional bug;
  • Understand, in a general sense, how the site is being read and used.

Nothing you send is used for advertising, resold to third parties, or shared beyond what's necessary to operate the site.

03Cookies & local storage

The Void does not use tracking cookies or advertising pixels. The site's browser may use minimal local storage purely for functional purposes — for example, remembering a reading preference within a session. This never identifies you personally and isn't shared with anyone.

04Third‑party services

A few outside services help this site function, and each has its own privacy practices worth knowing about:

  • Hosting (Netlify). The site is deployed and served through Netlify, which may process standard server logs.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts). Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive a request for the font files when a page loads.
  • Email & Instagram. If you contact The Void by email or Instagram DM, that conversation is subject to the privacy policy of that platform (Gmail / Meta) as well as this one.

05Your choices

You're always welcome to browse The Void without sharing any personal information at all. If you've previously emailed the site and would like your message or details removed from any records, just say so — see the contact box below.

06Children's privacy

The Void is not directed at children under 13, and the site does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared information with the site, please get in touch and it will be removed.

07Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally as the site grows or changes. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision. Meaningful changes will be noted here in plain language, not buried in legalese.

08Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request about your data? Reach out directly.
