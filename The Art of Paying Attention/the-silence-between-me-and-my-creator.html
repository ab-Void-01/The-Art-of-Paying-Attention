The Silence Between Me and My Creator

ASWords by The VoidJul 05, 2026

There's a particular silence I've come to know well — not the absence of sound, but the specific stillness that shows up when I stop talking long enough to actually listen. I used to fill that silence with requests, with questions, with the constant low hum of asking for things. Lately I've been practicing just sitting in it instead.

I don't always get an answer I can point to. No voice, no sign lit up in the sky. Just quiet, and me inside it, smaller than I am in most other rooms. I used to find that unsettling — silence felt like being ignored. Now I think I had the metaphor wrong. Silence isn't always absence. Sometimes it's just a different register of presence, one I hadn't yet learned to hear.

What I bring to the quiet

I bring my ordinary complaints, mostly. The things that went wrong, the things I'm afraid will. But increasingly I bring gratitude too, unprompted, for things far too small to justify prayer in the way I was taught to think of it — a stranger's small kindness, a morning that turned out gentler than I expected, the sheer improbable fact of being here at all to notice either one.

Maybe the silence isn't where the answer is missing. Maybe it's where I finally stop needing one immediately.

A relationship without proof

I don't have this figured out, and I'm suspicious of anyone who claims they fully do. But I've stopped needing the silence to resolve into something provable before I trust it. Some relationships work that way — built less on evidence and more on showing up quietly, again and again, and letting the showing up be its own kind of answer.

So I keep sitting in it. Not because I've solved anything. Because something about the quiet, held long enough, starts to feel less like waiting and more like company.

In the quiet, with you,
The Void
